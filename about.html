Design Philosophy

I design experiences that turn audiences into participants. I start by understanding the people who will use, play, or inhabit an experience, then use storytelling, interaction, and technology to give them meaningful ways to discover something for themselves. I prototype early, observe how people actually behave, and let those observations shape the experience.

The Manor

A Ghostbusters-inspired comedy-horror dark ride, where you are trained to find and release trapped ghosts and rescue an old manor. The goal of the project was to playtest an existing dark ride design, refine it based on our observations, and build the new version for the CMU Fall Festival.

Showcased at the 2025 CMU ETC Fall Festival.


ROLE Experience Design, Environment Design
TOOLS Unity, custom show control system

Space Provided

A 3-wall CAVE system that combines immersive, 3D projection with an active motion base.


An existing dark ride design to be playtested and redesigned.


A custom-made show control system and DMX lighting setup.


CAVE with moving floors and three 3D projection walls.



My Role

Designed the experience in a team of 5. Playtested with varying group dynamics and redesigned parts of the experience. Redid the environment layout based on our artist's design.

01

Playtesting

Playtested an existing dark ride project with 6 groups of varying dynamics. Documented findings and redesigned experience based on them.






02

Environment Redesign

Rearranged environment layout in Unity to match our artist, Melanie Danver's, new design.

× ❮ ❯

Final Experience

You're a rookie ghostbuster, tasked with finding five glowing orbs hidden throughout a haunted manor and releasing ghosts trapped in each zone by solving a cooperative puzzle.
